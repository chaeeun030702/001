건설회사의 공무팀장이셨던 아버지는 추석 당직 근무 날 저를 현장에 데려가신 적이 있으셨는데, 아버지는 현장의 정리정돈이 근로자의 안전을 지키는 여러 방법 중 하나라며 5S와 3정(정리, 정돈, 청소, 청결, 습관화와 정품, 정량, 정위치)을 설명해 주시며, 같이 일하는 동료들이 무사히 퇴근할 수 있도록 직원들이 노력하고 있다고 해 주신 말씀을 기억하고 있습니다. 어릴 때 꿈은 아픈 사람을 돌보는 간호사였지만, 그날 이후 다친 사람을 돌보는 것보다 근로자와 동료, 회사가 다치지 않도록 하는 일이 먼저라고 생각하게 되어, 고3 때 안전관리자를 목표로 안전공학과에 진학하였습니다.

코카콜라음료는 음료를 만드는 공장이라 설비와 제품의 이동, 원료와 포장재의 보관, 물류 동선이 한 사업장에서 이어집니다. 여주공장 안전관리자는 산업재해의 원인을 분석해 안전교육 계획을 세우고, 안전보건경영시스템(ISO 45001)을 운영하며 사업장 순회 점검까지 맡는 자리로 알고 있습니다. 위험을 찾는 일부터 교육과 시스템으로 이어 가는 일까지 한곳에서 배울 수 있다는 점에 끌려 지원했습니다. 올해 9월 한국 코카콜라가 여주의 '물 품은 숲' 495ha 관리 성과를 공유한 것을 보며, 지역 환경을 책임지는 사업장이라면 그곳에서 일하는 사람의 안전도 같은 무게로 지켜야 한다고 생각했습니다.

[Safety Manager Application — Coca-Cola Beverages Korea, Yeoju Plant]

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am applying for the Safety Manager position at the Yeoju Plant of Coca-Cola Beverages Korea. I am a senior majoring in Safety Engineering at Chungbuk National University, expected to graduate, and I would like to help the plant run its safety and health management system so that everyone goes home safely.

My career goal began with my father, a project administration manager at a construction company. While he was on duty during the Chuseok holiday, he took me to his site and explained that good housekeeping is one of the ways to protect workers, introducing 5S and the "3 Jeong" principles (right item, right quantity, right place). I had dreamed of becoming a nurse, but that day I decided that preventing injuries comes before caring for the injured, and in my last year of high school I chose Safety Engineering.

A beverage plant moves materials, packaging and finished products through many machines and people every day, and the work that most needs control is non-routine work such as maintenance, cleaning and inspection. In the Serious Injury and Fatality (SIF) case archive of Korea's safety agency, non-routine tasks accounted for 20.5% of 2,573 fatal accidents in manufacturing, and the leading causal factor, with 283 cases, was equipment that was not stopped and power that was not isolated. I would make lockout/tagout (LOTO) and isolation checks before maintenance and cleaning my first priority, and I would confirm that contractors follow the same procedure.

To prepare for this role, I completed 15 safety courses in English, including programs from the International Training Centre of the ILO, AIChE and OSHAcademy, and an ISO 45001:2018 course. I also completed five AI courses from Google, Microsoft and Anthropic. Using 30,896 accident cases from Korea's Construction Safety Management Information System (CSI) and related laws, I built a web application (e-safety.vercel.app) that generates multilingual risk assessments and Permit-to-Work (PTW) documents from a site photo, and the paper won the Top Excellence Award at the 2026 Korea Electrical Contractors' Association best paper competition. I am preparing for the practical exam of the Industrial Safety Engineer certification, and I am also preparing for the ISO 45001 Internal Auditor qualification and the NEBOSH IGC. My English (OPIc AL) was built through language study in Canada, work as an English conversation instructor and a summer engineering program at a U.S. university.

If I join the plant, I will start by learning how inspections, incident investigation and safety training are run in practice, and I will keep housekeeping (5S) and toolbox talk routines simple enough for every worker to follow. I will record near misses and track corrective and preventive actions (CAPA) until they are closed. Thank you for considering my application.

Sincerely, Chaeeun Lee
