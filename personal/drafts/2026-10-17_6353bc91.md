건설회사의 공무팀장이셨던 아버지는 추석 당직 근무 날 저를 현장에 데려가신 적이 있으셨는데, 아버지는 현장의 정리정돈이 근로자의 안전을 지키는 여러 방법 중 하나라며 5S와 3정(정리, 정돈, 청소, 청결, 습관화와 정품, 정량, 정위치)을 설명해 주시며, 같이 일하는 동료들이 무사히 퇴근할 수 있도록 직원들이 노력하고 있다고 해 주신 말씀을 기억하고 있습니다. 어릴 때 꿈은 아픈 사람을 돌보는 간호사였지만, 그날 이후 다친 사람을 돌보는 것보다 근로자와 동료, 회사가 다치지 않도록 하는 일이 먼저라고 생각하게 되어, 고3 때 안전관리자를 목표로 안전공학과에 진학하였습니다.

LX MMA는 울산공장에서 MMA와 이소부틸렌을 만드는 화학 회사입니다. 화학 공정은 설비와 물질이 한 번 흐트러지면 큰 사고로 이어질 수 있어, 공정안전 관리(PSM)가 생산과 떼어 놓을 수 없는 일이라고 배웠습니다. 올봄 미국화학공학회(AIChE)의 화학공정 안전과 폭주반응 과정을 이수하며, 위험은 사고가 난 뒤가 아니라 설계와 운전 단계에서 막아야 한다는 점을 익혔습니다. LX MMA가 ISO 45001을 운영하며 안전보건을 최우선으로 둔다는 점에서, 생산 현장에서 공정과 품질, 안전을 함께 배울 수 있다고 판단해 지원했습니다. 전공이 화학공학은 아니지만 공정안전 교육으로 기초를 쌓았고, 입사 후에는 선배에게 공정 흐름과 설비를 먼저 배우겠습니다.

다만 정확한 결론을 위해서는 원자료가 중요하다는 판단에 자료를 찾는 데 시간을 많이 써 일정이 지연되고 있었으나, 우선 찾은 data로 분석을 시작하고 부족한 부분은 추가 분석 시 최초 분석보다 시간이 적게 소요됨을 확인하여 보완하는 방식으로 바꿔 일정을 되돌렸고, 가진 자료로 먼저 시작해 고쳐 나가는 법도 배웠습니다.

**[Production (Ulsan) Application — LX MMA]**

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am applying for the production position at LX MMA's Ulsan plant. I am a senior majoring in Safety Engineering at Chungbuk National University, and I would like to start my career where process, quality and safety are managed together.

My path began with my father, a project administration manager at a construction company. While he was on duty during the Chuseok holiday, he took me to his site, explained the 5S and the "3 Jeong" principles (right item, right quantity, right place), and told me that good housekeeping is one of the ways his team made sure everyone went home safely. That day I decided that preventing injuries comes before treating them, and I chose Safety Engineering.

LX MMA produces MMA and isobutylene, where a single loss of containment or an uncontrolled reaction can escalate into a major accident. That is why Process Safety Management (PSM) cannot be separated from production. Although my major is not chemical engineering, I built a foundation in process safety by completing AIChE courses in Hazard Recognition, Chemical Process Safety, Runaway Reactions and Process Safety: Learning from Experience, together with Major Accident Prevention and Fire Safety Inspection from the International Training Centre of the ILO. In total I completed 15 international safety courses, all in English. Through them I learned to rank hazards by likelihood and severity and to control risks at the design and operating stages rather than after an incident.

Lacking field experience, I prepared my thesis aiming to be the person who had analysed the most real accident cases and countermeasures. I combined the Ministry of Employment and Labor's industrial accident analysis reports, 30,896 accident cases from Korea's Construction Safety Management Information System (CSI), 840 serious accident cases and safety regulations into a database, and completed five AI courses to analyse it. The analysis showed that foreign workers on Korean sites are increasing, that their per-capita occupational injury rate is 1.94 times that of all workers, and that most Korean safety systems rely on Korean-only documents. I then completed a web application (e-safety.vercel.app): from a photo of an electrical or general work site, AI trained on the database identifies hazards and automatically produces a hazard analysis, a risk assessment and a Permit-to-Work (PTW) in Korean with English, Chinese, Vietnamese and Uzbek. I then added a monitoring mode that alerts managers by e-mail and text message, and the paper won the Top Excellence Award at the 2026 Korea Electrical Contractors' Association best paper competition, held at the Korean Institute of Electrical Engineers (KIEE) autumn conference.

As LX MMA operates an ISO 45001 system and works with Japanese shareholders, I would contribute by reading and organising English technical standards and procedures.
